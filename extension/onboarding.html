Send to Learn

Capture anything worth reviewing, without opening the app.

1. Pin the icon so it is one click away

Browsers can't pin it for you, so:

  1. Chrome / Edge / Brave: click the puzzle-piece in the toolbar, find Obsidian Optimizer, click the pin.
  2. Firefox: right-click the toolbar and choose Customize, or open the extensions menu and pin it.

2. Sign in

Use your ObsidianOptimizer credentials. The API base is already set to https://obsidianoptimizer.uk/api.

3. Capture anything

Click the icon and press Capture this page, or right-click any selection, link, or page and choose Send to Learn. The extension works out the rest:

  • Plain text / markdown: saved as a note for review
  • A web page link: read and turned into notes
  • A YouTube / video link: downloaded to watch offline, plus notes
  • A PDF / audio / video link, or a dropped file: saved to your workspace, plus notes

Everything lands in the Learn page: media to watch or read, and generated notes in your Inbox to review, file, and edit.
